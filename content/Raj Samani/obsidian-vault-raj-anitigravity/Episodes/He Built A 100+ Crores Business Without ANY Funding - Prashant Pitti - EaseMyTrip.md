## Key Insights & Takeaways

- Throwing money at the problem never solves
the problem
- We have never seen a quarter of a loss, while our competitors have never seen a quarter
of a profit
- He never took any money from any VC
- If you want to build a business from your
house, and want to take it to IPO, and if you want to make more than 100 crores
profit, then do watch till the end
- You know, we never had this eye-grabbing headlines,
right
- So, you never reached out to PR because it wasn't organic
- Which is what I think a lot of bootstrap founders probably would also find it very hard
- I think the usual ones, MakeMyTrip, ClearTrip, Yatra, XCO, IBBO, they all pre-existed
- See, I think that, you know, VC money is a great money to solve teething problems
- You will never tell your friend that I know a website which charges less convenience fees
- Convenience fees are one of the biggest ways to make money
- In fact, there is a huge difference in profitability that in the last 14 years of our existence
which is 56 quarters, we have never seen a quarter of a loss while
our competitors have never seen a quarter of a profit
- And you are telling me like they have never
seen profits
- I can't say their names but yes that's what
it is that none of our competitors have made money and while we have always been profitable since
day one
- Exactly because you are always at cost cutting
- It's actually throwing money at the problem
never solves the problem
- It's just that we were always pushed to the
corner
- We were always considered as an underdog and
nobody really cared as much
- Here if you make a mistake, it's a huge cost
to pay
- And by telling him that you know you are not
alone, 30% people make such mistakes, you are actually saving his ego
- He can go home and show his face that I am
not the biggest L in the world
- And that is why I think E-Smart Trip grew
primarily because most of our competitors did not want to own this
experience
- I have never heard of that ever before
- Instead of getting, we were getting 2-3 thousand
calls a day at that time, I think we started getting 10,000-12,000 calls
a day
- I think that shows a lot about entrepreneur
and the kind of principles and values you come from and what you have
in life
