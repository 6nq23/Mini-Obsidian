# Ankush Sachdeva: Building MOJ and Sharechat into Unicorns

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Ankush Sachdeva: Building MOJ and Sharechat into Unicorns from Ankush Sachdeva (Sharechat / Founder & CEO). Key focus on India-First Product Philosophy, Network Effects Mastery.

## Guest Overview
Ankush Sachdeva is a leading entrepreneur and business leader at Sharechat / Founder & CEO. Their expertise spans building scalable businesses, navigating market challenges, and creating sustainable growth strategies in the Indian and global markets.

## Key Insights

### India-First Product Philosophy
- Sharechat built for India's vernacular internet
- Short-form video aligned with Indian consumption patterns
- Regional language content was differentiator vs TikTok
- Understanding local user behavior shaped product

### Network Effects Mastery
- Creator ecosystem drives content quality
- Algorithmic feed increases engagement and retention
- Platform effects create defensible moat
- Monetization follows engagement at scale

### Scaling Challenges
- Content moderation at massive scale
- Balancing creator freedom with safety
- Regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions
- Maintaining growth while improving profitability

### Monetization Paths
- Creator monetization programs
- Advertising from local brands
- Gifts and direct payments
- E-commerce integration opportunities

## Frameworks & Mental Models
- **Network Effects**: The most powerful force in scaling businesses - more value attracts more users
- **Unit Economics**: Sustainable business requires positive unit economics from inception
- **Product-Market Fit**: Essential before scaling; determines fundraising ceiling

## Notable Quotes
> "India doesn't need copies of global apps, it needs apps built for India."
> "Network effects are the most powerful force in tech."
> "Short-form video is the most engaging content format globally."

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs / Builders
- Building for local language opens massive markets
- Network effects compound faster than technology advantages
- Creator monetization enables sustainable platforms
- India-specific solutions can outcompete global players
