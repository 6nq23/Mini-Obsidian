# Deep Kalra: Building Profitable Tech Businesses in India

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Deep Kalra: Building Profitable Tech Businesses in India from Deep Kalra (MakeMyTrip / Founder). Key focus on Profitability First Philosophy, Market Understanding.

## Guest Overview
Deep Kalra is a leading entrepreneur and business leader at MakeMyTrip / Founder. Their expertise spans building scalable businesses, navigating market challenges, and creating sustainable growth strategies in the Indian and global markets.

## Key Insights

### Profitability First Philosophy
- Built sustainable business focusing on unit economics from day one
- Avoided VC trap of growth at all costs
- Maintained 30%+ EBITDA margins even during scaling
- Strategic acquisition and consolidation vs pure organic growth

### Market Understanding
- India travel market had specific needs (fragmented suppliers)
- Discovery and aggregation were the core value propositions
- B2C customer acquisition expensive, B2B margins better
- Cross-selling opportunities across flights, hotels, holidays

### Business Model Strategy
- Commission-based model with suppliers (hotels, airlines)
- Dynamic pricing and yield management
- Technology as competitive moat
- Scale of operations improves supplier relationships

### Capital Efficiency
- Raised capital strategically, not opportunistically
- Maintained control through bootstrapping initially
- Exit strategy: successful IPO and acquisitions
- Capital allocation discipline across product lines

## Frameworks & Mental Models
- **Network Effects**: The most powerful force in scaling businesses - more value attracts more users
- **Unit Economics**: Sustainable business requires positive unit economics from inception
- **Product-Market Fit**: Essential before scaling; determines fundraising ceiling

## Notable Quotes
> "Profitability is not the opposite of growth, it's the prerequisite."
> "Unit economics don't lie. If your unit economics are bad, no amount of scale will save you."
> "India needs its own solutions, not just copies of Western models."

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs / Builders
- Build defensible unit economics before scaling aggressively
- Market-specific solutions outperform global templates
- Capital discipline enables long-term value creation
- Technology advantages compound as scale increases
