# Future Technology & AI: Web3, Metaverse & Opportunities

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** The 2020s mark a pivotal shift where digital world engagement surpasses physical world interactions. Getting early into emerging technologies like Web3, metaverse, and AI can create wealth. Focus on understanding and investing in technologies that seem niche today but become mainstream in 10 years.

## Guest Overview
Sai Krishna V K is a technology investor and AI expert who has built and invested in cutting-edge technology ventures, including exits worth $20-30 million. Known for deep expertise in emerging technologies including Web3, cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and the metaverse.

## Key Insights

### Digital World Supremacy
The current decade (2020-2030) represents the first time in human history where digital world engagement is more important than physical world engagement. Understanding this shift is fundamental to making strategic investments and building relevant businesses.

### Getting Early Into Trends
Successful entrepreneurs and investors don't chase trends—they identify meaningful technologies 10 years ahead and build when others are skeptical. Early believers in crypto, web3, and metaverse technologies are positioned to capture generational wealth.

### The Metaverse & NFT Opportunity
The metaverse, NFTs, and cryptocurrencies represent paradigm shifts in how humans interact, own assets, and create value. These technologies move from speculation to practical utility, creating opportunities for both investors and builders.

### Technology as Wealth Creation
People making millions and billions didn't work on what was popular at the time; they worked on something that was relevant 5-10 years in the future. This requires conviction and patience to get early into technologies that seem distant today.

### Cryptocurrency as an Accessible Entry Point
Cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum democratize investment opportunities. Starting with investments as small as ₹100 allows ordinary people to participate in wealth creation through digital assets.

## Frameworks & Mental Models

- **Early Technology Adoption Framework**: Identify technologies that solve real problems today but might become mainstream in 5-10 years. Invest time and capital with conviction before mass adoption.
- **Digital-First Thinking**: Build and think in digital-first terms. Physical world considerations are secondary in the 2020s.
- **Wealth Creation Through Foresight**: Success comes from believing in something early and getting deeply involved before others see the opportunity.

## Notable Quotes

> "What makes 2020 to 2030 different from any other time in human history is that spending time in a digital world is way more important than spending time in a physical world."

> "You need to believe in something and get early in order to make that kind of money and wealth."

> "If you look at people who have made billions and millions, they worked on something which was relevant 10 years ago and now it has become mainstream."

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs & Builders

- Develop strong conviction about emerging technologies before they hit mainstream adoption
- Get early into platforms and technologies that solve real problems today
- Build expertise in Web3, AI, and digital assets as these will shape the next decade
- Think digital-first in all business and investment decisions
- Participate in crypto markets to understand digital finance mechanics
- Network with builders and investors already working in frontier technologies
