## Key Insights

### Market Opportunities
- Hotmail founded in 1997, revolutionized email access from anywhere
- Web-based email was novel—email clients required desktop installation
- Microsoft acquisition happened when innovation was accelerating
- First-mover advantage and simplicity drove adoption
