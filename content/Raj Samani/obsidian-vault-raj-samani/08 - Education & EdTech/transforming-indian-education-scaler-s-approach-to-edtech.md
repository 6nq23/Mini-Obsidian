# Transforming Indian Education: Scaler's Approach to EdTech

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Scaler is transforming Indian education by teaching practical tech and business skills to working professionals. Their model focuses on placement outcomes and real-world relevance over traditional credentials.

## Guest Overview
Anshuman Singh is the founder of Scaler, an online education platform transforming how Indians learn technology and business skills. Scaler focuses on working professionals seeking career growth, with emphasis on practical skills and placement outcomes.

## Key Insights

### EdTech Model Innovation
Scaler's model differs from traditional colleges: focus on employment outcomes, practical skills, mentorship, and peer learning. Success is measured by placements and salary growth.

### Skill-Based Learning
Content is built around what industry needs, not what curriculum says. Updated frequently based on job market demands.

### Accessible Quality Education
Online format makes quality education accessible to tier-2 and tier-3 cities where colleges are of lower quality. Cost is lower than traditional colleges.

### Placement Focus
Every course is designed with employment outcomes in mind. Partnerships with companies ensure students learn relevant skills and have job opportunities.

### Community & Peer Learning
Cohort-based learning creates peer network and accountability. Students learn from each other, not just instructors.

### Affordable & Flexible
Students work while learning. Flexible schedules and installment payments make education affordable for middle-class Indians.

## Frameworks & Mental Models
- **Outcomes-Driven Education**: Define desired outcome → Design curriculum → Teach relevant skills → Mentor students → Measure placement → Iterate
- **Community Learning Model**: Individual learning + Peer support + Mentorship + Industry partnerships = Better outcomes than solo learning
- **EdTech as Career Transformation**: Skill building → Mentorship → Placements → Salary growth → Career acceleration

## Notable Quotes

> "We measure success by placements and salary growth, not degrees."

> "The future of education is skill-based, not credential-based."

> "Building a supportive peer community is as important as good instructors."

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs & Builders
- Focus on outcomes, not just content delivery—placements matter more than credentials
- Build curriculum based on job market needs, not academic tradition
- Peer learning and community are as important as individual study
- Mentorship from working professionals accelerates learning
- Practical projects and real-world problems beat theoretical exercises
- Regular curriculum updates keep content relevant to industry changes
- Industry partnerships ensure students learn what companies actually need
- Accessibility and affordability enable social mobility through education
