**Rohit Gupta** from Education Counselor shares insights on choosing the right college: avoiding fake institutions & scams. The episode explores key business principles, challenges, and opportunities in this domain.

## Key Insights

### Market Opportunities
- Top 100 universities are non-government funded by private players
- Faculty quality is biggest differentiator between colleges
- Infrastructure and labs determine practical learning quality
- Counselling industry often recommends based on commissions

Understanding the strategic decisions and business models that drive success in this field.

## Frameworks & Mental Models

- **College Evaluation**: Look at faculty, infrastructure, placements
- **Red Flags**: Fake colleges make unrealistic promises
- **Network Value**: Alumni network and peer quality matter
- **Counselling Scams**: Be skeptical of commission-driven recommendations

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs & Builders

- Ask for placement data and contact actual alumni
- Faculty expertise matters more than institution age
- Visit campus and talk to current students before enrolling

## Actionable Takeaways

1. Identify your specific market opportunity and validate it with real customers
2. Build a team that complements your skills and brings diverse perspectives
3. Focus on profitability from day one rather than just growth metrics
4. Stay updated with industry trends and technological changes
5. Create value for your customers consistently before worrying about scaling
