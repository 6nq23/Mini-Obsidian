## Frameworks & Mental Models

- **Customer Obsession**: Design every system around customer benefit, not company profit
- **Simplification**: Remove complexity from processes, not from thinking
- **Long-term Planning**: Reinvest profits for 5-10 year growth rather than quarterly results
- **Operational Excellence**: Build systems that scale without proportional cost increases

## Notable Quotes

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs & Builders

- Focus on customer benefit, even if it hurts near-term profitability
- Build scalable systems before you need them
- Simplification applies to products, processes, and decisions

## Actionable Takeaways

1. Identify your specific market opportunity and validate it with real customers
2. Build a team that complements your skills and brings diverse perspectives
3. Focus on profitability from day one rather than just growth metrics
4. Stay updated with industry trends and technological changes
5. Create value for your customers consistently before worrying about scaling
