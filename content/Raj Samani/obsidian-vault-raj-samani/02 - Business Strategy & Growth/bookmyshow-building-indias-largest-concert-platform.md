# BookMyShow: Building India's Largest Concert Platform

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** BookMyShow: Building India's Largest Concert Platform from Ashish Sharma (BookMyShow / Founder). Key focus on Event Economics & Scale, Business Model Evolution.

## Guest Overview
Ashish Sharma is a leading entrepreneur and business leader at BookMyShow / Founder. Their expertise spans building scalable businesses, navigating market challenges, and creating sustainable growth strategies in the Indian and global markets.

## Key Insights

### Event Economics & Scale
- Organized India's largest concert (Coldplay) with 700+ crores economic impact
- Live events now contribute 40% of revenue (vs 60% from movies)
- Strategic choice of Ahmedabad as concert venue (no alcohol, unique market opportunity)
- Total ticket revenue was managed through bot-protected queue systems

### Business Model Evolution
- Survived complete restructuring - reduced from 150 to 6 employees during crisis
- Pivoted to live events as revenue growth engine
- Diversified from movies into concerts, sports, and entertainment
- Artist management and touring represents new growth vector

### Operational Excellence
- International supply chain coordination (materials from Israel, Georgia, Southeast Asia)
- Concurrent stage setups for multi-city tours
- Complex logistics for transporting 150,000+ attendees
- Infrastructure coordination with government agencies and railway ministry

### Customer Experience Design
- Premium experience through Xylo bands (RF wristband technology)
- Artist-centric approach to event production
- Pricing strategy: cheapest tickets globally ($80 for premium seats) to maximize access
- No alcohol venue didn't diminish experience quality

## Frameworks & Mental Models
- **Network Effects**: The most powerful force in scaling businesses - more value attracts more users
- **Unit Economics**: Sustainable business requires positive unit economics from inception
- **Product-Market Fit**: Essential before scaling; determines fundraising ceiling

## Notable Quotes
> "When somebody tells you no, it really excites me. It excites me to no extent."
> "We want to give the opportunity to as many people. Our tickets were the cheapest anywhere in the world."
> "Rather than putting out your phone and not enjoying that experience, you don't need to do that."

## Lessons for Entrepreneurs / Builders
- Embracing constraints (no alcohol, cricket stadium) creates differentiation
- Government partnerships enable scaling beyond private sector limits
- Premium experiences command scale and justify operational complexity
- Artist-first approach builds loyalty across the event ecosystem
