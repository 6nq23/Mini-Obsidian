# He Is Transforming INDIAN Education System Ft. Scaler Founder Anshuman Singh

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Anshuman Singh's Scaler is transforming Indian education by teaching practical tech and business skills to working professionals. Their model focuses on employment outcomes and real-world relevance over traditional credentials, making quality education accessible and affordable to tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

Anshuman Singh is the founder of Scaler, an online education platform that's fundamentally changing how Indians learn technology and business skills. Unlike traditional colleges that focus on degrees, Scaler measures success by placement outcomes and actual salary growth of its students.

## Key Insights

### EdTech Model Innovation: Outcomes Over Credentials
Scaler's model differs fundamentally from traditional colleges. Instead of measuring success by degrees awarded, they track placement rates and salary growth. Every course is designed backward from employment outcomes—identifying what companies need, then building curriculum around those skills. This focus on outcomes creates accountability that traditional education lacks.

### Skill-Based Learning Aligned with Industry Demands
Content is built around what the job market actually needs, not what academic curricula prescribe. The curriculum is updated frequently based on real-time job market demands. When companies start hiring for a new technology, Scaler's instructors—themselves working professionals—quickly incorporate it into courses. This keeps learning relevant and immediately applicable.

### Accessible Quality Education for Underserved Markets
Online format makes quality education accessible to students in tier-2 and tier-3 cities where local colleges offer lower-quality instruction. Cost is significantly lower than traditional colleges, and flexible payment options (installments) make it affordable for middle-class Indians who work while studying.

### Placement-Centric Design and Industry Partnerships
Every course is designed with employment outcomes as the primary goal. Partnerships with companies ensure students learn exactly what employers need and have job opportunities upon graduation. This creates a virtuous cycle: companies get trained employees, students get jobs, and Scaler builds credibility and revenue.

### Community and Peer Learning as Competitive Advantage
Cohort-based learning creates a peer network and mutual accountability. Students learn from each other, not just from instructors. This peer support system is as important as the quality of instruction—it keeps students motivated and creates long-term networks.

### Affordability and Flexibility for Working Professionals
Students can work while learning thanks to flexible schedules and installment payment options. This removes barriers to entry for talented individuals from lower-income backgrounds who might otherwise be locked out of quality education.

## Frameworks & Mental Models

## ✅ Practical Action Steps

> [!tip]
> These are concrete actions you can take TODAY based on this episode.

1. **Identify Your Actual Market Opportunity**: Don't build based on assumptions. Talk to 50+ potential customers about their pain points and desired outcomes. Document what they actually need vs. what they're currently taught.

2. **Design Backward from Outcomes**: Start with the end goal (job placement, salary increase, skill mastery) and work backward. What does a student need to learn to achieve that outcome? What sequence makes sense?

3. **Build Industry Partnerships Early**: Connect with companies before launching your product. Understand their hiring needs, get them involved in curriculum design, and ensure they'll hire your graduates. Don't assume jobs exist.

4. **Create Peer Learning Communities**: Whether online or offline, facilitate peer-to-peer learning. Assign cohort-mates, create study groups, enable knowledge sharing. This multiplies the impact of your instruction.

5. **Make Quality Education Affordable**: Explore flexible payment options, installment plans, or income-based pricing models. Remove financial barriers for talented people from low-income backgrounds—they often become your most motivated students.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

> [!note]
> Core lessons from this episode.

- **Outcomes beat credentials**: Success metrics should be job placements and salary growth, not degrees awarded or student satisfaction scores.

- **Curriculum should follow industry demand, not tradition**: Update courses frequently based on real job market needs. What companies hire for today is more important than what colleges have taught for decades.

- **Peer community creates accountability**: Cohort-based learning and peer networks are as valuable as instructor quality—maybe more so for adult learners.

- **Online education solves geographic inequality**: Quality instruction can now reach tier-2 and tier-3 cities that lack good colleges. This is a major social impact opportunity.

- **Mentorship from practitioners accelerates learning**: When your instructors are working professionals with real-world experience, teaching is dramatically more relevant and credible.

- **Flexibility enables working professionals to upskill**: Flexible schedules and installment payments remove barriers for people who need to work while studying. This opens education to millions.

- **EdTech is fundamentally disrupting traditional education**: The combination of accessibility, affordability, and outcomes-focus makes traditional colleges vulnerable. The disruption is just beginning.
