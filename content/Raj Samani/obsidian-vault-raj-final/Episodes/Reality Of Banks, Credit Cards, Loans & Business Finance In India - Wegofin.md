# Banking, Credit & Business Finance in India

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Indian banking system has expanded credit availability through digitization but significant gaps remain. Fintech companies like Wegofin are bridging gaps by using technology and alternative data (mobile behavior, transaction history) to serve underbanked customers and small businesses that traditional banks overlook. Credit is now being accessed in minutes vs. weeks; speed and accessibility are competitive advantages.

## Guest Overview

Wegofin founder created a fintech platform making credit more accessible to Indians. The platform uses technology and alternative data sources to assess creditworthiness beyond traditional CIBIL scores, serving underbanked customers and small businesses that traditional banks overlook.

## Key Insights

### Banking System Evolution
- **Digital expansion**: Indian banks have expanded credit availability through digitization
- **Alternate data sources**: Moving beyond traditional CIBIL scores to assess creditworthiness
- **Gap identification**: Traditional scoring methods still exclude millions; opportunity exists

### Credit Access Challenge
- **Underbanked population**: Many Indians lack credit history to qualify for bank loans
- **Small business struggle**: SMBs struggle to access affordable credit with traditional banks
- **Fintech role**: Technology companies addressing the accessibility gap

### Alternative Credit Scoring
- **Beyond CIBIL**: Using mobile data, transaction history, behavioral patterns to assess creditworthiness
- **Accessibility advantage**: Reaches customers banks can't serve with traditional scoring
- **Risk assessment**: Enables lenders to assess real risk beyond old-fashioned metrics

### Loan Processing Speed
- **Traditional timeline**: Bank loans take weeks to approve and disburse
- **Fintech advantage**: Technology-enabled platforms process applications in minutes to hours
- **Streamlined processes**: Automation and reduced human touchpoints improve speed significantly

### Interest Rates & Economics
- **Risk-based pricing**: Credit costs based on risk assessment; higher-risk = higher rates
- **Profitability challenge**: Finding right risk-return balance determines fintech profitability
- **Sustainability**: Unsustainable rates lead to defaults; sustainable rates enable growth

### Regulatory Framework
- **RBI oversight**: Reserve Bank regulations protect consumers but can slow innovation
- **Compliance navigation**: Fintech must navigate regulations carefully while building products
- **Regulatory evolution**: Regulators learning about fintech business models

## Frameworks & Mental Models

- **Lending Risk Model**: Customer assessment → Risk scoring → Interest rate determination → Collection strategy
- **Digital Lending Value Chain**: Application → Verification → Approval → Disbursement → Repayment → Analytics
- **Fintech Advantage Stack**: Speed + Accessibility + Better UX + Lower costs = Customer preference

## ✅ Practical Action Steps

> [!tip]
> Five specific actions for fintech lenders:

1. **Understand customer borrowing needs**: Not all loans are the same; segment customers carefully
2. **Set sustainable interest rates**: Reflect actual risk; unsustainable rates lead to defaults and business collapse
3. **Prioritize collections**: Repayment management is as important as lending quality
4. **Build data rigorously**: Technology enables speed, but cannot replace assessment rigor
5. **Comply from day one**: Regulatory compliance is not optional; build it into systems from inception

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

> [!note]
> Seven core lessons on fintech lending:

- **Technology enables speed**: Digital platforms process loans in hours vs. weeks; speed is competitive advantage
- **Alternative data unlocks market**: Behavioral data can predict creditworthiness better than credit scores
- **Risk and profitability linked**: Set rates based on actual risk; race to the bottom destroys profitability
- **Collections = profitability**: Lending quality matters; collections execution determines ultimate success
- **Customer education critical**: Borrowers must understand loan terms, obligations, and costs
- **Regulation is moving target**: Engage with regulators early; compliance builds trust
- **Data security paramount**: Financial data breaches destroy customer trust and business
