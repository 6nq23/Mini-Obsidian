# BookMyShow Founder: Building India's Largest Concert Platform

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** BookMyShow transformed India's event landscape by hosting the country's largest concert (Coldplay in Ahmedabad). Success came from embracing constraints, government partnerships, artist-centric operations, and scaling logistics for 700+ crore economic impact events. Live events now drive 40% of revenue, proving the viability of entertainment infrastructure in India.

Ashish Hemrajani is the founder of BookMyShow, India's largest ticketing and entertainment platform. He has scaled the company through major pivots, surviving near-bankruptcy during the pandemic to build India's largest concert with Coldplay, generating 700+ crores in economic impact.

## Key Insights

### Event Economics & Scale
- Organized India's largest concert (Coldplay) with 700+ crores economic impact
- Live events now contribute 40% of revenue (vs 60% from movies)
- Strategic choice of Ahmedabad as concert venue (no alcohol, unique market opportunity)
- Total ticket revenue was managed through bot-protected queue systems
- Xylo bands (RF wristband technology) created premium immersive experience without phone distractions

### Business Model Evolution
- Survived complete restructuring - reduced from 150 to 6 employees during crisis
- Pivoted to live events as revenue growth engine
- Diversified from movies into concerts, sports, and entertainment
- Artist management and touring represents new growth vector

### Operational Excellence
- International supply chain coordination (materials from Israel, Georgia, Southeast Asia)
- Concurrent stage setups for multi-city tours
- Complex logistics for transporting 150,000+ attendees
- Infrastructure coordination with government agencies and railway ministry
- Managed bot-protection and fair ticket distribution at scale

### Customer Experience Design
- Premium experience through Xylo bands (RF wristband technology)
- Artist-centric approach to event production
- Pricing strategy: cheapest tickets globally ($80 for premium seats) to maximize access
- No alcohol venue didn't diminish experience quality; artist curation matters more
- Enabled artist-fan connection through immersive, phone-free experience

### Infrastructure as Moat
- Government partnerships enable scaling beyond private sector limits
- Public infrastructure spending drives concert feasibility
- Stadium access (Narendra Modi Stadium) requires relationship building and negotiations
- Creating demand for live entertainment itself is the competitive advantage

### Building Festivals for Emerging Artists
- Festivals are mechanism for giving Indian artists big stages
- Major international acts legitimize Indian music talent on global scale
- Tourism impact and economic spillover from concerts extends beyond ticket revenue

## Frameworks & Mental Models

> [!important]
> **Constraint-Driven Innovation**: Embrace constraints (no alcohol, cricket stadiums) as differentiation vectors
- **Network Effects**: More artists attract fans; more events attract artists; virtuous cycle builds
- **Unit Economics**: Premium experiences command higher margins and justify operational complexity
- **Government as Partner**: Infrastructure-heavy businesses scale faster with public sector alignment

## ✅ Practical Action Steps

> [!tip]
> Concrete actions you can take TODAY.

1. **Map Constraint Opportunities**: Identify constraints in your market as differentiation vectors. What can't competitors do that you can embrace as a feature?
2. **Build Government Relationships Early**: For infrastructure-heavy businesses, start stakeholder mapping with government agencies, not VC. Infrastructure partnership accelerates scale.
3. **Study Event Economics**: If building in events/ticketing, analyze unit economics per attendee. Premium experiences command higher margins and justify complex operations.
4. **Create Artist/Vendor First Experiences**: Design for your supply side (artists, vendors) as much as demand side (customers). Happy creators drive network effects.
5. **Invest in Logistics Tech**: If scaling live events, bot-protection, queue management, and fair distribution are competitive moats. Invest in these unglamorous technologies.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

> [!note]
> Core lessons from the episode.

- **Constraints create differentiation**: Embracing "no alcohol" venue, cricket stadium, or geographic limitations can become competitive advantages if executed well
- **Business model resilience matters**: Surviving 90% downsizing and pivoting from movies to live events demonstrates adaptability as critical skill
- **Live events are infrastructure play**: Success requires coordination across supply chains, government, artists, and logistics—not just consumer technology
- **Government partnerships > pure private sector**: Infrastructure-heavy businesses scale faster with public sector alignment than fighting regulations
- **Premium experience justifies complexity**: Willingness to invest in artist curation, immersive tech (Xylo bands), and operational excellence enables premium pricing and scale
- **Artist-centric beats consumer-centric**: Focusing on artist satisfaction and experience drives fan loyalty more than chasing volume of attendees
- **Underserved markets have overlooked economics**: Ahmedabad concert proved demand exists outside metro bubble; regional focus opens blue ocean opportunities
