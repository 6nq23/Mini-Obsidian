# European Billionaire In India: Marketing, Leadership, Hiring & Odoo

> [!abstract]
> **TL;DR:** Europe's high tax burden (77% in Belgium) makes India attractive for scaling. Offline marketing is underrated. Incompetent middle management destroys teams. Bad managers serve themselves; good leaders serve their teams. India has cheap resources and can become a global power if it prioritizes efficiency and quality.

He brings a unique perspective on building a European-born company, the challenges of operating in high-tax environments, and why India represents both an opportunity and a testing ground for global expansion.

## Key Insights

### Insight 1: The Economics of Operating in Europe vs. India
- **Belgium tax burden**: 77% of gross income goes to the government as taxes
- **High-tax motivation for India**: This high tax burden makes India attractive for lower-cost operations and higher margins
- **Ability to sell globally**: Sell to US and European markets at higher prices with low Indian cost base = sustainable competitive advantage
- **India's unrealized potential**: India has not yet understood the power of its cheap resources + global market access
- **Implication**: European companies are using India as the cost base to serve global markets

### Insight 2: The Competency Crisis in Indian Management
- **Most middle managers incompetent**: A systemic problem, not individual failures
- **Bad manager characteristics**: Treats the team as servants of the manager; focuses on personal benefit
- **Good leader characteristics**: Positions themselves as servants of the team—coaching, training, developing people
- **Impact on team**: Incompetent management destroys team morale, motivation, and retention
- **Opportunity**: Companies that train quality managers have competitive advantage

### Insight 3: Underrated Offline Marketing
- **Digital marketing dominates discourse**: Google Ads, KPIs, etc. are all measurable and visible
- **Offline marketing severely underutilized**: Billboards, events, in-person have high impact but hard to measure
- **Europe experience**: Offline marketing works exceptionally well in Europe but is underutilized
- **Optimal approach**: Mix measured digital performance with unmeasurable but high-impact offline
- **Results**: Combining digital precision with offline impact delivers better overall results than pure digital

### Insight 4: Why Zoho Has No Serious Competitors
- **Zoho's competitive advantage**: Built efficiency and quality at scale
- **No Indian competitor has matched**: Zoho's ability to think big and execute globally
- **Zoho's success**: Didn't need Silicon Valley validation to succeed
- **Implication**: Indian companies can build global-scale businesses without US capital or validation

### Insight 5: Building Global-Quality Products in India
- **India's potential**: If Indians understood how to become "really really efficient," India could become a global superpower
- **Current challenge**: Balancing low costs with world-class quality
- **Efficiency applies everywhere**: Product, operations, customer service, marketing
- **Competitive advantage**: Efficiency at global quality levels is hard to replicate

## Frameworks & Mental Models

> [!important]
> **Tax Arbitrage Model**: Low-cost operations in high-tax-friendly countries + global market access = sustainable margins. Use this for profitability.

- **Manager vs. Leader Spectrum**: Rank your team leadership on a scale from "serves themselves" to "serves the team." Invest in moving people toward service.
- **Marketing Channel Mix**: Don't rely solely on measurable digital. Factor in unmeasurable offline impact for brand building.
- **Efficiency as Competitive Advantage**: Treat efficiency (in product, ops, service, marketing) as a core competitive advantage, not just cost reduction.

## ✅ Practical Action Steps

> [!tip]
> Concrete actions you can take TODAY based on this episode.

1. **Audit your management quality**: Identify your 5 worst-performing managers. For each, assess: Do they serve themselves or serve their teams? Create a development plan to shift them toward service or replace them.

2. **Develop middle management talent**: Middle management is a leverage point. Allocate budget for manager training, coaching, and development. A better manager multiplies team effectiveness 2-3x.

3. **Audit your marketing spend**: List all marketing channels (digital and offline). For digital, measure ROI precisely. For offline, estimate impact qualitatively. Are you over-investing in measurable digital and under-investing in high-impact offline?

4. **Calculate your cost arbitrage**: If you're in a high-tax country, calculate the tax burden. Consider moving operations (especially R&D, support, delivery) to lower-tax countries. Use India as your cost base.

5. **Build for global markets, not local**: Start by serving global markets from day one, not just your local market. Use India's cost base to be profitable globally.

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

> [!note]
> Core lessons from this episode.

- **Leverage India's Cost Advantage**: Build profitable global businesses by using India's cost structure as your base for global markets.
- **Invest in Developing Quality Middle Management**: Middle management is a leverage point. Quality managers multiply team effectiveness. This is not optional.
- **Don't Over-Rely on Performance Marketing**: Use offline for brand building and awareness. Combine with digital for full-funnel approach.
- **Think Globally from Day One**: Build for export markets, not just India. Use India's economics to be profitable at global prices.
- **Understand That Efficiency is a Competitive Advantage**: Not just cost reduction, but efficiency across product, ops, service, marketing. This is hard to replicate.
- **Study Zoho's Playbook**: Global ambition, execution-focused, no VC dependency. Proven model for Indian companies.
- **Focus on Manager Quality**: Bad management is the #1 destroyer of teams. Good management is the #1 multiplier of effectiveness.
